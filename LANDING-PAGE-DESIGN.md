# LogMyDose Landing Page Design

## Overview

This document outlines the structure, copy, and design direction for the LogMyDose marketing landing page. The page is designed for users seeking a simple way to track any health protocol—GLP-1s, peptides, HRT, supplements, and more.

**Primary goal**: Waitlist signups (pre-launch) → App downloads (post-launch)

**Secondary goal**: Establish trust and differentiation from competitors

**Key positioning**: LogMyDose is substance-agnostic—it tracks any protocol. GLP-1s are featured as the primary example (market entry point), but the app works for peptides, HRT/TRT, supplements, and custom protocols.

**Important**: All copy emphasizes that LogMyDose is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. We do not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice.

Q: What can I track with LogMyDose?

A: LogMyDose is purpose-built for medication tracking. You get one-tap

Q: Can I import my existing data?
A: Yes! You can import from Apple Health, upload a spreadsheet (CSV/Excel),

Q: Is my data private?
A: Absolutely. Your data is stored securely and never shared or sold. You

Q: Do I need to get my medication through you?
A: No. LogMyDose works with medication from any source—telehealth providers,

Q: Should I use this instead of talking to my doctor?
A: No—LogMyDose is meant to help you prepare for those conversations, not
   replace them. Think of it as a way to bring organized notes to your
   appointments. Always work with your healthcare provider for medical
   decisions.

Q: What if I want to cancel?
A: Cancel anytime with one tap—no questions asked. You can export all your

## Design Direction

### Color Palette

| Use | Color | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Primary | Deep teal or calm blue | Trust, health, calm |
| Accent | Soft coral or amber | Warmth, energy, CTAs |
| Background | Off-white / light gray | Clean, modern |
| Text | Dark charcoal | Readable, professional |
| Success | Green | Positive indicators |

### Typography

- **Headlines**: Modern sans-serif (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or similar)
- **Body**: Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Tone**: Helpful but not clinical; warm but not casual

### Visual Style

- Clean, minimal UI with generous whitespace
- App mockups showing organized tracking (not medical interfaces)
- No stock photos of smiling people with needles
- Subtle gradients, soft shadows
- Mobile-first design (most users will arrive on mobile)

### Imagery

- Product screenshots (hero, features)
- Simple icons for features
- Abstract patterns/gradients for section backgrounds
- No generic health imagery

## Mobile Considerations

- Hero: Stack vertically (headline, mockup, CTA)
- Comparison tables: Scrollable or convert to stacked cards
- Features: Single column, swipeable
- Pricing: Stacked cards
- FAQ: Accordion works well on mobile
- Sticky bottom CTA bar on scroll

## Key Legal Considerations

1. **No medical claims**: Never imply diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice
2. **User empowerment language**: "Track," "organize," "share with your doctor"
3. **Avoid AI-forward messaging**: Focus on organization, not intelligence
4. **Clear disclaimers**: Medical disclaimer in FAQ, footer, and solution section
5. **No comparative health claims**: Don't reference statistics about user outcomes
6. **Export/data ownership**: Emphasize user control over their data
